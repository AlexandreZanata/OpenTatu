`benchmark/results/*.json` — do not edit by hand. How it works:
[benchmark/README.md](README.md) and ADR-0031.

- **Found**: known flaws matched by a reported finding (confirmed, not dismissed).
- **Hypothesis only**: matched only by a hypothesis that was not reported.
- **Outside the list**: reported findings that match no known flaw — the false-positive
  candidates. Apps mark only part of their flaws (Juice Shop marks its coding
  challenges, not every challenge), so many of these are real: review them before
  calling them false positives.
- Recall = found / known; list precision = reported findings that match / reported.
